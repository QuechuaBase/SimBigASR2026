SIMBig 2026 · Arequipa, Peru · 28–30 October 2026

Call for Participation

Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) and Language Resources for Indigenous Languages of South America

Submissions are closed. The results were announced at SIMBig 2026; see the results page. This page summarises the call. The official version is on simbig.org.

About the shared task

This shared task promotes speech technologies and open resources for Indigenous languages of South America, starting with Puno Quechua. It encourages reproducible, open research and collaboration with Indigenous language communities.

Tasks

Task 1

Puno Quechua ASR

Build an ASR system using the Mozilla Common Voice Puno Quechua datasets (scripted and spontaneous speech). Baseline models and evaluation scripts are provided. Systems are ranked by WER on a held-out test set, and must be reproducible.

Data & baselines · Results

Task 2

ASR for any South American Indigenous language

Develop an ASR system for an Indigenous or native South American language that does not yet have one. Teams must share their train/dev/test splits, and the test set must be at least 30 minutes long.

Outcome

Task 3

Educational and literacy resources

Create a resource, such as a website, tutorial, notebook, visualisation, video or learning platform, that helps people learn or explore an Indigenous language, or reuse its datasets.

Review and publication

Each paper was evaluated by at least two independent reviewers on originality, technical quality, significance and relevance. All manuscripts were screened with plagiarism detection software. Papers from the winning teams will be published, after revision in response to reviewer feedback, in the Springer Communications in Computer and Information Science (CCIS) series.
